# Texture and mesh roles (modern_vfx)

The full per-id library (names, flipbook settings and mesh sizes) is in `assets.md` and `assets/catalog.tsv`. This file explains which families do which job.

**How this was made:** asset thumbnails were viewed through `thumbnails.roblox.com/v1/assets?assetIds=...&size=150x150&format=Png`, rendered as a grid on that origin in the browser pane. The full machine list (class, id, uses, flipbook layout, effects, emitter names) is regenerated by `tools/texscan.lua`.

## What the textures tell you
- **Most particle textures are flipbook sheets** (Grid4x4/Grid8x8/Custom, OneShot, 16–64 fps). They are animated splashes, smoke puffs, fire licks, spark bursts and impact stars. A modern effect **plays an animation per particle** instead of tweening a static blob, so set `FlipbookLayout` and `FlipbookMode=OneShot` whenever the texture is a sheet. The motion is inside the texture.
- **The texture families, and what each is for:**
  - **Soft radial glow:** the bloom under everything. Examples: 1075864321 "glow", 107821950776778 "Fading Glow", 129922860680208 "Glow", 13189667061 "Glow".
  - **Thin uneven rings and crescents:** shockwave edges. Examples: 6862984716 "HardRing/ThickRing", 18838828771, 91195065145907 "ripple01".
  - **Star glints / 4-point flares:** a sparkle accent on peaks. Examples: 131311043057562 "Star", 96013523226630 "Flare Twinkles", 99621248183954 "simple glowing star".
  - **Stylized cel smoke** with hard-edged flat-shaded puffs: the residue. Examples: 16867522149 "Stylized Smoke59", 624634365.
  - **Wind slashes / streaks:** motion lines. Examples: 10859315454 "winds", 17409991165 "best wind", 11820076505 "StylizedWind1", 15380148205 "Wind".
  - **Fire sheets:** 135866425386690 "Fire11b", 16860219678 "Fire32", 16860226897 "Fire33", 122954463046434 "Fire125".
  - **Impact stars:** 17265977573 "Impact2/Hit", 78043674430474 "Impact4", 8048608283 "StylizedImpact1/FatImpact".
  - **Water sheets:** 16865854535 "Water7", 16865899680 "Water10", 16865902191 "Water11", 16866055035 "Water27", 16866193510, 16866200449, 17510992903 "waterflipbook", 13795304468 "Breaking Splash", 95364728062846 "Water Splatter", 123266658426840 "Erica Water Wave Blur 8x8".
  - **Debris:** 11353163220 "rockflip2" (rock chunks, Grid2x2), 85858177391804 "stylized leaf".
- **Textures are authored white or greyscale.** Hue comes entirely from `Color`, so one texture serves every element. A few (fire 119058761936819, the rainbow flares 101943315608494 / 78772401055219) carry their own color and are used as accents.
- **Black-background flares** (70728001087698, 10556525755, 17511015942) rely on additive blending (LightEmission 1), where the black adds nothing.

## Beam textures (strips, read along the length)
Wispy smoke strips (18925205019, 18470709110), torn energy/lightning ribbons (12781855094, 85772363699785, 72969225620485), braided swirl patterns (100013157062966, 131025768925255), a twisting curl (124753331461910), vertical streak clusters (13530571563) and fire (119058761936819).
- **Torn edges and gaps are the point.** With `TextureSpeed` animated (Forge `Speed_Texture_*`), the gaps scroll along, so the beam reads as moving air or energy rather than a solid tube.

## Decal textures (wrapped onto meshes)
Radial spike bursts (9581481497, 9572542664, 14277319792, 9622368924), swirl and vortex rings (88983666349740, 84938636872942, 10423614733, 14261953171, 15249897265), crescent-fragment rings (15190416756, 108886089477305), a soft ring (74266965397559), a thin ring (18280512172), a radial flower burst (14101024770) and vertical streaks (13530571563).
- **On a mesh, the decal supplies the detail** (broken edges, streaks) **and the mesh supplies the 3D shape and perspective.** The mesh itself stays Transparency 1. See `emit_patterns.md` (decal-on-mesh).

## Meshes (by shape role)
- **Torn spiral or wind ribbons:** the modern "wind ring". These are partial, broken, twisted bands, never a closed torus. Examples: 4681227436, 113322248153294, 6351288405, 74181482131152, 113793875136271, 129778117978668, 7211026250 (BrightFrontWind), 76812637992657.
- **Thin wavy or tilted rings:** shockwaves that aren't perfectly flat. Examples: 5922911630, 87273433428492, 7430299023 (Meshdown), 7472102456 (flat thin ring).
- **Vertical hoops:** side-on rings for dash and impact planes. Examples: 8089863097, 18434669670.
- **Open bands, short cylinder walls:** expanding ground rings. Examples: 95063569401865, 12070899434 (redmesh2).
- **Cones, funnels, flared vases:** upward bursts and tornado bases. Examples: 103992871225921, 125349505454888, 117825344749393, 7423457269 (a flat cone, for ground spikes).
- **Domes, hemispheres, bowls, spheres:** shells and blast bubbles. Examples: 8501563708 (wind6 dome), 122638957149603, 136867373491158, 137603874842453, 9729834399 (lens disc).
- **Columns and twisted cylinders:** pillars and tornados. Examples: 106381405601872 (twisted), 137685697016553, 128408217404396 (twisted blob), 119075613348711 (crumpled wrap).
- **Torn shell and debris:** 71849885439764 (crumpled shell), 102470267627424 (spike crown), 117547468732069 (ground tile chunks), 7722530552 (torus).

**Mesh rule:** the modern look uses **broken, asymmetric geometry**: torn ribbons, wavy and tilted rings, twisted columns. A closed, perfect sphere, ring or cylinder is the outdated look. When a primitive is used, it carries a broken decal, or it lives for 1–3 frames.
